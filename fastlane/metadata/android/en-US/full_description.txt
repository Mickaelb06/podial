Podial Player brings podcasts and live radio together in one free app. No account, no tracking, no Google dependency.

<b>Podcasts</b>
• Top charts by country and by genre (all Apple genres and sub-genres), search, subscriptions
• Offline downloads, sleep timer, playback speed, played-episode tracking
• New-episode badges and notifications
• Published transcripts, with quotes you can save and share as an image card

<b>Live radio</b>
• Radios by country and city, by genre and sub-genre, and webradios grouped by brand
• The official local "ici" stations (ex-France Bleu) by region
• Station logos, the track now playing, and a world map with day and night
• A "surprise me" button to discover a random station

<b>Also</b>
• Android Auto support
• Landscape mode while playing: a spinning vinyl with the station logo or cover, the time and the weather (the weather uses your approximate location only if you allow it)
• Home-screen widgets, including a vinyl that spins while playing

Podcast data comes from the public iTunes API and radios from radio-browser.info.
